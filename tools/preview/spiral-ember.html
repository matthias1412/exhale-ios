The inner dots are mud

Measured off the real day-90 capture: at design radius 46 and 54 the dots render as pure background — fully hidden. At 62 they are #603E21 and at 70 #A77128: brown, at 38% and 65% brightness. The first dot at full brightness sits at radius 78. Cause: the centre veil is solid to 58 and fades to 82, but dots are placed from 44 — so roughly the innermost sixth of every spiral is painted over at partial opacity. It is a brightness problem, not a hue one. Recolouring does not fix it; not placing dots under the veil does.
