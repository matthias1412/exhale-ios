What you see when you open the app on the day you cross a mark. It has to feel like a reward and then hand off to the spiral behind it — the dot it leaves is where that moment lives permanently, so a burst that just fades has thrown the meaning away.

2 · The breathing orb — second batch

The first six were one idea in six textures: a ring of dots that scales. These are six different mechanisms. Scale is only one way to show a breath and probably the weakest — it is hard to read at a glance, and it leaves the four-second hold visually dead, which is exactly when someone is staring hardest. Every one of these keeps something moving through the hold. Scrub to 0.28 for the top of the inhale and 0.75 for the bottom of the exhale.
