Exhale — how a day arrives

Seven takes on the one you picked: a day is born at the numeral and travels out to its slot. Each changes one property of that — where it starts, how far it unwinds, how it paces, how much comet it drags. All run off one shared clock, so drag the scrubber to freeze the same instant across every panel; that is where they separate. Try it at 5y, where three of them fall apart.
