Exhale — motion options

Click any panel to replay it. Same phyllotaxis maths as the app, same colour ramp. Timing and easing are the real values — this is for picking a direction, not for pixel-matching; the device is still where it gets signed off.

1 · The spiral arriving

Why the current one feels flat: it is a fade-in with a 5.5% inward drift. I wrote that drift deliberately small because larger values "turned into visible churn" at 1,800 dots — but at every count below a few hundred it reads as nothing happening. The variants below make the arrival mean something without churning: the spiral unwinds into place, or a visible edge sweeps outward and dots land behind it.

2 · The milestone burst

Currently a ring of dots expands and fades while text appears under it. The ring never does anything the eye can follow, and the dot that is supposed to hand off to the spiral just vanishes.

3 · The breathing orb

The real problem: this whole app is made of dots, and the orb is the one thing that isn't — it is a blurred radial gradient, which is why it reads as a smudge rather than as part of the same object. The alternatives are built from the app's own dots.
