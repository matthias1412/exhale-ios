Exhale — the mark

The spacing is right and the sizing is not. Dots sit an even 2.6–2.9 units apart all the way out, which is correct phyllotaxis — but the diameter grows outward, so the clear space between neighbours falls from 101% of a dot at the centre to 11% at the rim, with the tightest pairs 0.03 units apart. A bloom reads through its spiral arms, and the arms are choked exactly where they would be most visible. Every variant below is measured, not guessed.
